# H1 sweep paper rules

Paper only. No Cornix. No Telegram. No live orders. Public Bitget USDT-perp 1m and 1H klines.

## Session

03:05–09:11 America/New_York. Time-stop at the 09:11 ET mark.

## Short

1. Previous hourly candle closed bullish.
2. Current hour takes that candle's high (sweep).
3. Accumulation held in the 20 minutes before the new hour (that high not already taken).
4. After the sweep, a bearish M1 FVG prints (candle 3 high < candle 1 low, middle candle bearish).
5. Short when a later M1 bar taps that FVG.

## Long (mirror)

Previous H1 closed bearish, current hour sweeps that low, bullish M1 FVG (candle 3 low > candle 1 high), long on the tap.

## Size and exits

- $100 margin, 10x isolated, $1,000 notional.
- Fee 0.06% per side ($1.20 round trip).
- Stop just beyond the sweep wick, plus 0.05% buffer. Skip if that stop is wider than 2% of price.
- Target at least 2R. If the opposite side of the dealing range is closer than 2R, skip. Cap 4R.
- One open trade per symbol. Longs and shorts both allowed.
