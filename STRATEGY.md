# Public ICT check vs the slides we shipped

The user asked to look up the public @ICT carousel **“The Only Strategy You Need for 2026”** (7 slides) and say whether it matches the drawn slides (session 03:05–09:11 NY, previous H1 close, current-hour sweep, M1 AMD + FVG tap).

## Finding

The public materials that use that title, or that ICT students treat as “the only strategy,” **do not match** the 03:05–09:11 H1-sweep carousel. Per the instruction, this engine implements the **slides**, not the public models.

## Sources

1. **YouTube — “one system or trading strategy that you can follow in 2026” (fractal model)**  
   https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9AL41xON3hA  
   Daily bias from the prior daily close / sweep, then a higher-timeframe point of interest (FVG or swing), candle-2 or candle-3 closure, drop to a paired lower timeframe, enter a **continuation order block**, target **2R**. No 03:05–09:11 window. No “previous H1 must be bullish then take that high” rule.

2. **ICT Silver Bullet (the usual public “only strategy” time model)**  
   https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-silver-bullet-strategy/  
   https://crypoptionhub.com/what-is-ict-silver-bullet/  
   Three **one-hour** New York windows: 03:00–04:00, 10:00–11:00, 14:00–15:00. Sweep + FVG inside that hour. Not a six-hour 03:05–09:11 session, and not gated on the previous hourly candle’s close.

3. **ICT 2022 model / FVG definition (the three-candle gap itself matches)**  
   https://www.tmgm.com/en/academy/trading-academy/ict-trading  
   https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/fair-value-gap-trading-strategy/  
   Bearish FVG: candle 3 high below candle 1 low. Bullish: candle 3 low above candle 1 high. Stop beyond the sweep wick. That **definition** matches the slides. The session clock and the “previous H1 close then current hour takes that extreme” sequence do not.

No public Instagram carousel on the official ICT handles reproduced the exact 7-slide 03:05–09:11 text. Fan posts titled “only strategy” collapse to Silver Bullet or a generic HTF-bias → sweep → FVG flowchart, not this hourly model.

## What we shipped (the slides)

- Session only 03:05–09:11 America/New_York.
- Short: previous H1 closed bullish; current hour takes that high; M1 accumulation, upward manipulation, bearish FVG; short on the tap.
- Long: the mirror.
- Stop just beyond the sweep wick (+0.05% buffer), never wider than 2% of price.
- Target ≥2R, skip if the opposite dealing-range side is closer, cap 4R.
- Time-stop at 09:11 ET.
